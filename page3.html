User Stories

As a customer:

  • create an account and log in so that they can manage their bookings.
  • view cat profiles so that they can learn about the cats before visiting.
  • browse the food and drink menu so that they can see what the cafe offers.
  • book an available session so that they can spend quality time with the cats.
  • update or cancel their booking so that they can adjust their visit when their plans change.

As an administrator:

  • manage sessions so that they can control visiting times and capacity.
  • view and manage bookings so that they can organise customer visits.
